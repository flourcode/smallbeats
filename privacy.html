Privacy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Short version: Small Beats has no accounts and no ads. It uses Google Analytics to count visits. Your tapes stay in your browser.

What's stored, and where

The tapes you save, the mix you're working on, your volume and the name you put in FROM are kept in your browser's local storage on your device. We never receive them. Clearing your browser's site data removes them.

If you add Small Beats to your home screen, your browser also keeps a copy of the site so it opens offline.

Tape links

A tape link carries the tape itself: the parts, a number for the tune, the name, the sticker, and the FROM name if you added one. Anyone you send it to can see those. Nothing about the tape is stored on our side.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to see how many people visit and how often people press play, save a tape, send one or open one. It sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*) to tell visits apart, except in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, where it runs without cookies. Google Analytics doesn't store IP addresses, advertising features are off, and we don't combine this with anything else. If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, Small Beats doesn't load Google Analytics at all. Google's policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Services your browser talks to

Besides this website, only Google Analytics. The music is made on your device, and the fonts and stickers are served from smallbeats.com.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses and pages requested) for security and reliability. We don't use them to identify or track visitors.

Email

If you write to us, we'll use your email only to reply.

Children

Small Beats doesn't ask anyone for personal information, including children.

Changes

If any of this changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
