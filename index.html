ABOUT

What is this?

A tiny music machine. Pick a starting sound, turn parts on and off, and when it sounds right, put a sticker on the tape, name it and send it to somebody. They open the link and hear your tape. Then they can remix it and send it back.

No account, nothing to install, nothing to learn. If you don't like the tune, hit NEW TUNE and it writes another one with the same parts.

How does it work?

Nothing you hear is a recording. The drums, bass, keys and the rain outside are all made in your browser while you listen, and every part follows the same chord changes, so whatever you switch on still fits together.

A tape link is a short recipe: the parts you picked, a number for the tune, the name and the sticker. The other person's browser plays it from that.

Who made it?

One person who grew up making mixtapes off the radio. It's free, with no ads.
