ABOUT

What is this?

A tiny music machine. Pick a style, tap the parts to change them, play along on the keys, and when it sounds right hit SAVE TAPE. EJECT lets you name it, pick a buddy for the label and send the link. They open it and hear your tape. Then they can remix it and send it back.

No account, nothing to install, nothing to learn. If you don't like the tune, hit NEW and it writes another one with the same parts. UNDO takes it back.

How it works

  • Nothing is a recording. Drums, bass, keys, rain and fire are synthesized in your browser while you listen, a bar at a time.
  • Everything fits. Every part follows the same chord changes, so whatever you switch on plays in key.
  • A tune is a number. A tape link holds the parts, the buddy and a number for the tune, so everyone who opens it hears the same song.
  • Real instruments, in code. Rhodes is FM synthesis, the guitar is a plucked-string model, 8-bit is pulse waves, and the drums are noise and oscillators: soft, lo-fi, house and dub kits, played TIGHT or with SWING. The keyboard is a little synth voiced for each style.
  • The cassette chain. Lo-fi tapes run through tape saturation, wow and flutter, a gentle roll-off and a little hiss.
  • No AI. Not one note is AI-generated.

Questions

Can I use the music in my videos?

Yes. The music is royalty-free: use it in videos, streams, podcasts, games or anything else, even commercially, with no fee and no need to ask. Credit is appreciated, not required. Because anyone can make the same tape, please don't register it with Content ID or resell it as a sample library. The details are in the terms.

Can my tape play on Small Beats FM?

Yes. Drop its link in the comments under any Small Beats FM video. Tape Mail picks from there, and every tape that plays is credited with the MIXED BY name on it.

Can I download a tape as a file?

Not as a file. The music is made live, so to keep it, record it: your phone or computer's screen recorder works, and so does OBS for longer recordings.

Is any of it AI?

No. Not one note is AI-generated. Everything is synthesized by code in your browser, from the tape's settings and a number for the tune.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to install. It's free, with no ads.

Where are my tapes kept?

In this browser, on this device. Your shelf link (under YOUR TAPES) holds every tape in one link: open it in any other browser or phone to bring them along. Private browsing windows forget everything when they close.

Does it work offline?

Yes, once the page has loaded. Add it to your home screen and it opens like an app. On iPhone it plays even with the ring switch on silent.

Which browsers does it work in?

Current Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, on computers, phones and tablets.

What it does

  • Six styles. Lo-fi, House, Ambient, 8-Bit, Dub and Trance, each written fresh from a seed.
  • Six parts. Drums, bass, keys, texture, speed and volume. Tap one to change it, and it changes on the beat.
  • NEW. A new tune in the same style, landing on beat 1. Hold it to spin the name.
  • The keyboard. Every key fits the song. Hold for long notes. Play along while the music plays and what you play joins the song.
  • The band. Fifty pixel buddies live on the screen, react to what you press, and sing when you poke them. Dino hosts.
  • Mini Tapes. SAVE TAPE puts it on your shelf. EJECT to name it, pick a buddy and a shell, and send the link.
  • Something to watch. A scene for every style, weather from the texture key, a sky that follows your clock, and the odd visitor.
  • Royalty-free. Every note is made in your browser, so the music is yours to use, even commercially. How it works.
