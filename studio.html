STUDIO

Build a broadcast address for OBS or a screen recording: the whole machine playing the night by itself (the missed train, six stops, the last train home), Dino and the crew on the screen, NOW PLAYING and MIXED BY credits, a QR code to play the tape yourself (16:9), and now and then a little hand playing along, so viewers see it's an instrument they can play. Pick tapes from your shelf or paste links people sent you.

THE NIGHT
ROUTE

One long night, a new route every day. The route number is only for you: it never appears on screen, in titles or in descriptions. The whole night plays when the playlist is exactly the route's six tapes, in order.

THE ROUTE'S SIX TAPES

Made by the machine from the route: one tape per stop, in order, each one answering what its stop needs. Minor keys, mostly slow. The same six for anyone who builds this route.

Leave it as is to use this site. Use https://smallbeats.com/ if you opened the Studio from a copy on your computer.

In OBS:
  1. Add a Browser source and paste the address.
  2. Set width 1920 and height 1080.
  3. Tick Control audio via OBS so the music goes into the recording or stream.

To record without OBS, open it in a new window, go full screen (F11, or Ctrl+Cmd+F on a Mac) and click once if it asks to start the sound.
